10th August.

The best day I remember.

The day I first fell in love with you.

A day I'll carry with me,
and never forget.

I overthought every little thing.

I turned small fears into big ones,
and doubts into answers.

Somewhere along the way,
my overthinking hurt something
that was once beautiful.

And I hate that I couldn't quiet my mind
before it became too loud for us.

The sun never leaves the sky out of anger.

It only knows the hour it was given.

Some lights are not put out,
they are only carried elsewhere.

So I open my hands,
and let the evening take
what I could never keep.

Go gently, wherever your morning finds you.

One last thing…
Thank you for being a beautiful part of my life.
